How to Set Up Slack Phone & Desktop Alerts

Follow these 3 quick steps to receive instant scratch notifications in your Slack mobile or desktop app:

  1. 1
    Go to api.slack.com/apps → click Create new app → select Blank app. Name it Fantrax Assistant, select your workspace, and click Create App.
  2. 2
    In the left sidebar under Features, click Incoming Webhooks and toggle Activate Incoming Webhooks to On.
  3. 3
    At the bottom under Webhook URLs for Your Workspace, click Add New Webhook. Select your personal Direct Messages → @YourName (or a private channel) so only you see your alerts, click Allow, and copy the generated Webhook URL into the field below!
